# Home photographic instruments

Created for this project with the built-in `image_gen` tool, with transparent backgrounds. The PNG alpha channels are preserved.

- Camera: `public/images/home/rangefinder-camera-photo.png` (1536 × 1024).
- Microphone: `public/images/home/vintage-microphone-photo.png` (1024 × 1536).

These are photographic bitmap assets animated in layers, rather than full 3D meshes. The camera lens and aperture are separate animated layers; the microphone and its cable share a suspended pivot with a damped response to scroll. No purple lighting is used.

## Camera prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Production website photographic cutout asset, not a mockup. One single extremely photorealistic premium compact rangefinder camera with classic silver satin-metal top and bottom plates and pristine finely pebbled black leather body, modern understated styling like a Fujifilm X100 camera without brand names or logo. Beautiful round moderately projecting black machined lens centered slightly to the right of body center, etched small clean photographic lens markings, fine concentric metal and rubber focus rings. The FRONT OPTICAL GLASS of the lens must be a perfect circle viewed exactly straight on, deep near-black with subtle blue-green optical coating and small realistic pale softbox reflections near its perimeter. The central 50% of lens glass should be almost black and empty so the website can animate an aperture there. Two restrained dark rectangular viewfinder windows, top exposure dials, tiny credible screws, clean silver machining. Absolutely straight-on front product elevation, level, no image tilt, no yaw, very little top visible. Whole camera fully framed and isolated, centered in a landscape composition with generous true transparent margin. High-end macro product studio photography with controlled broad soft light upper left, soft narrow right rim, excellent black material separation, natural metallic gradient and crisp tiny surface textures. Photographic, tangible luxury campaign quality, absolutely NOT a low-poly or toy-like render or illustration. Black and neutral silver, no gold, no purple, no magenta, no decorative glow. No strap, no hand, no base, no floor, no text overlay, no watermark. True transparent alpha background, no checkerboard drawn into image. High resolution.

## Microphone prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Production website photographic cutout asset, not a website mockup. One single exceptionally photorealistic classic 1950s chrome broadcast microphone, Shure 55 silhouette, unbranded. Upright rounded rectangular capsule with dense curved horizontal polished nickel grille ribs, visible intricately woven dark steel acoustic mesh inside, narrow central spine, realistic metal thickness and dark gaps. Rounded beautifully formed upper and lower shell, elegant small U-shaped polished chrome yoke with knurled side pivots, lower metal socket and short black cable connector. NO base, NO long stand, NO trailing cable, NO wire above: these are added separately by the site. Almost front view with a very slight 8-degree three-quarter angle showing the right side, not tilted in the image. Whole object, isolated, perfectly centered, generous transparent margin, vertical product photo. High-end macro studio photography, neutral silver chrome with convincing gradients, controlled slim softbox reflections, minute machining marks, subtle surface roughness, believable shadows inside grille. A very faint warm amber edge reflection only. Deeply photographic, tangible, luxury product campaign. Neutral silver and black, absolutely no purple or magenta, no neon, no decorative glow, no text, logo or watermark. True transparent alpha background, no ground, no backdrop, no checkerboard drawn into image. High resolution.
